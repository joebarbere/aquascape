# F4.3 — Grouping and "grow into each other" blending

**Stage:** 4 — Layers & Planting Tool
**Owner:** `scene-model-engineer`
**Collaborators:** `renderer-engineer`, `angular-feature-engineer`, `test-engineer`
**Status:** Not started

## Goal

Plants within a group blend into each other visually — overlapping foliage merges into a continuous mass rather than reading as separate sprites. Useful for carpeting plants and dense mid/background clusters.

## Spec reference

Plan Stage 4 F4.3.

## Dependencies

**Requires:** F4.1 (plants exist), F4.2 (layer concept).
**Enables:** Realistic-looking planted scenes; sets up Stage 4 visual quality bar.

## Scope

**In:**

- `PlantGroup` concept inside a layer: a named sub-collection of plant objects. Conceptually nested but flat in serialization: each PlantObject carries an optional `groupId: string` (UUID); a layer carries a `groups: { id, name, blendStrength }[]` list.
- Commands: `CreateGroup`, `AddToGroup`, `RemoveFromGroup`, `SetGroupBlendStrength`, `DissolveGroup`.
- Renderer extension: when drawing a group, render its plants to an offscreen canvas, then composite onto the main canvas with a blur+threshold pass that merges nearby silhouettes ("metaball-style" but cheap — Gaussian blur + alpha threshold approximation works at v1 quality).
- `blendStrength` controls the blur radius (0 = no blending, 1 = aggressive merge).

**Out:**

- True botanical leaf-level rendering — out for v1.
- Cross-group blending — groups blend internally only.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Plants in a group with `blendStrength > 0` visually merge in overlapping regions.
- [ ] A group of one or zero plants renders identically to ungrouped plants.
- [ ] Group commands are invertible.
- [ ] Group rendering performance: 50 plants in a group at default `blendStrength` renders in <16 ms on mid-tier hardware (sample with profiling).

## Testing

- **Unit:** group commands; blend math (blur radius from strength).
- **Snapshot:** rendered group with known plants + blend matches reference.
- **Component:** group create/dissolve from the layers panel.

## Notes

- **Composite per-group, not per-layer.** Layer-level blending would force every object to participate. Group-level is opt-in and lets users mix grouped masses with sharp individual plants.
- **Perf escape hatch.** If blur passes are too slow on a given machine, render groups without blending and surface a "reduced quality" indicator. Don't drop frames.
