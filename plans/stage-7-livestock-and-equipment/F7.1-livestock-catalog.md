# F7.1 — Livestock catalog

**Stage:** 7 — Livestock & Equipment
**Owner:** `catalog-engineer`
**Collaborators:** `angular-feature-engineer`, `test-engineer`
**Status:** Not started

## Goal

Add fish / shrimp / snails to the design with metadata: adult size, temperament, temp range, pH range, schooling minimum, bioload coefficient.

## Spec reference

Plan Stage 7 F7.1.

## Dependencies

**Requires:** F2.4 (catalog loader).
**Enables:** F7.2 (stocking guidance), F7.4 (setup sheet).

## Scope

**In, in `domain/catalog/`:**

- `LivestockCatalogEntry`:
  - `id`, `version`, common name, scientific name.
  - `kind`: `'fish' | 'shrimp' | 'snail' | 'other'`.
  - `adultSizeMm`.
  - `temperament`: `'peaceful' | 'semi-aggressive' | 'aggressive' | 'fin-nipper' | 'predatory'`.
  - `tempRangeC: [number, number]`, `phRange: [number, number]`.
  - `schoolingMin?: number` (for schooling species).
  - `bioloadCoefficient`: arbitrary unit for F7.2 calculations.
  - `tankZone`: `'top' | 'mid' | 'bottom' | 'all'` for UI grouping.
  - Visual: thumbnail + optional decorative sprite for rendering in the scene.
- Core catalog with 30+ common species.

**In, in `domain/scene-model/`:**

- `LivestockObject` extends `SceneObject` with a `count` field (since livestock often go in groups). Per the plan, livestock are "optionally rendered as decorative sprites; excluded from scape-only exports."
- New commands: `AddLivestock`, `RemoveLivestock`, `SetLivestockCount`.

**Out:**

- Live behavior simulation — out of scope.
- Compatibility checks — F7.2.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] At least 30 species with verifiable specs.
- [ ] Schema validates and rejects malformed entries.
- [ ] LivestockObject persists and round-trips.

## Testing

- **Unit:** schema validation; LivestockObject command invertibility.
- **E2E:** add a school of tetras, save, reopen — count preserved.

## Notes

- **Provenance.** Cite sources. Differences between sources (e.g. minimum tank size) are common — pick conservative numbers and document.
