# F5.3 — Guidelines, focal markers, alignment guides, distance readouts

**Stage:** 5 — Templates & Precision Guides
**Owner:** `renderer-engineer`
**Collaborators:** `angular-feature-engineer`, `test-engineer`
**Status:** Not started

## Goal

Pro-level composition aids: golden-ratio overlays, rule-of-thirds overlays, focal-point markers, smart alignment guides between objects, live distance readouts.

## Spec reference

Plan Stage 5 F5.3.

## Dependencies

**Requires:** F0.2 (golden-ratio / thirds helpers already in `domain/geometry`), F0.4 (renderer).
**Enables:** F5.4 (snapping uses these guides).

## Scope

**In, in `renderer-2d`:**

- Overlay layer (drawn on top of everything, not part of the scene):
  - Golden-ratio lines (φ-based vertical + horizontal).
  - Rule-of-thirds lines.
  - Focal-point markers at the four golden focal positions.
- Smart alignment guides (drawn during a transform drag):
  - When the dragged object's center / edge aligns with another object's center / edge or with a thirds/golden line, draw a thin highlighted line and the distance to the aligned object.
- Distance readouts: when an object is selected, render its position (mm) and bounding-box dimensions (mm) near the selection.

**In, in UI:**

- Overlay-visibility toggles in the View menu / panel.
- Persist toggle state per user (via StorageService).

**Out:**

- Overlays in exported images — they're **non-printing**.
- 3D overlays — defer to Stage 10.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All overlays toggle independently.
- [ ] Overlays do not modify the scene or document — they're a renderer-only concern (verified by a serialization test that overlay state never appears in `.aqua`).
- [ ] Alignment guides appear during drag and disappear on drop.
- [ ] Distance readouts update at 60fps during a drag.
- [ ] Overlays respect zoom level (lines stay 1 CSS px regardless of viewport zoom).

## Testing

- **Unit:** golden-ratio + thirds line position math (already in `domain/geometry`); guide-detection tolerance.
- **Snapshot:** rendered overlay at known viewport matches reference.
- **E2E:** drag an object, observe alignment guide appears at the φ line, drop, save — overlays don't serialize.

## Notes

- **Don't conflate overlays with scene objects.** Overlays render in the renderer's "decoration" pass after the scene; they have no `Layer` or `Command`.
- **Tolerance.** Alignment guides snap-show when within ~4 CSS px of an aligned position. Actual snapping (the cursor jumping) is F5.4's job.
