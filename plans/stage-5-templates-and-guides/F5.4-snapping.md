# F5.4 — Snapping

**Stage:** 5 — Templates & Precision Guides
**Owner:** `angular-feature-engineer`
**Collaborators:** `renderer-engineer`, `scene-model-engineer`, `test-engineer`
**Status:** Not started

## Goal

Snap moved/rotated/scaled objects to guides (golden / thirds), to a grid, and to other objects' centers/edges. Toggleable globally; with a modifier key (Cmd/Ctrl) to temporarily disable per-drag.

## Spec reference

Plan Stage 5 F5.4.

## Dependencies

**Requires:** F5.3 (guides exist), F3.3 (transform commands).
**Enables:** Precision composition.

## Scope

**In:**

- Snap targets:
  - Grid: configurable (default 10 mm minor / 50 mm major); align to nearest grid intersection when within tolerance.
  - Guidelines: golden / thirds — align object center or edge to a guideline when within tolerance.
  - Object-to-object: align centers / edges horizontally or vertically.
- Tolerance: 4 CSS px on screen (zoom-aware, converted to world mm).
- Implementation: a `snap(point: Vec2, context: SnapContext): Vec2` pure helper in `domain/geometry`. Transform handlers (drag / rotate / scale) consume it.
- UI toggles for each snap-target kind, plus a master "Snap" toggle.
- Holding Cmd/Ctrl during a drag temporarily disables snapping; releasing re-enables.

**Out:**

- Snap during programmatic transforms (e.g. paste) — defer; manual transforms only.
- Snap to substrate profile — out for v1; doesn't make composition sense.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All snap-target kinds work and toggle independently.
- [ ] Tolerance is consistent at different zoom levels.
- [ ] Cmd/Ctrl disable works during any drag.
- [ ] No snapping in exported images / unaffected views.

## Testing

- **Unit:** `snap()` pure helper with table-driven cases (grid, guide, obj-edge).
- **Component:** UI toggles persist; modifier key works.
- **E2E:** drag an object near a φ line, observe snap.

## Notes

- **Snap resolution order.** Object-to-object > guideline > grid (most specific wins). Document and test the precedence explicitly.
- **Visual feedback** for an active snap = the alignment guide line from F5.3 highlighting brighter. Coordinate the colors.
